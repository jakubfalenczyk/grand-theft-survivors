## Outstanding licensing issue

rawAmbience remains **not cleared for integration**. Previously inspected [general terms](https://rawambience.com/terms-conditions/) allowed games but included broad restrictions on redistributing material as-is, including within a larger work, and referred to purchases. They did not establish the free pack's exact terms for browser delivery. A royalty-free tag alone does not resolve this. Obtain the specific license before use.

Mayra's newer description and older comment differed about attribution. Retain the downloaded package terms; author credit is planned either way. ObsydianX and SnakeF8 can receive voluntary credits even if their selected license does not require them.
